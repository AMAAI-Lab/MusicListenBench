Can audio LLMs hear the difference yet?

Each test item is two short clips and one question about melody, rhythm, timbre or harmony. Every item comes in two variants: in FLIP the music changes and the answer switches, in STAY only the sound changes (room echo, EQ, hiss, transposition) and the answer stays. A model that ignores the audio scores 50%.
